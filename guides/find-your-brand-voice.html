BRAND BUILDING

Find a brand voice that sounds like you.

By Pocket AI · Creator’s Notebook

Start with people, not adjectives

Think about who you serve and what they need to feel: understood, confident, excited, or reassured. Then write down how you would explain your offer to one of those people in a real conversation. Your natural language is often a better starting point than a list of fashionable brand words.

Choose three useful principles

For example: clear, not technical; warm, not overly familiar; confident, not exaggerated. Pair each principle with a sentence you would use and one you would avoid. Concrete examples make a voice guide usable by teammates and AI tools.

Build a small language library

Collect five headlines, five calls to action, and five support replies that sound right. Note recurring words, sentence lengths, and how you address the reader. Also record language you avoid, especially unsupported claims and empty superlatives.

Review it in the real world

Use your guide on a landing page, a social post, and an email. Does the voice still feel like the same person while fitting each context? Ask a customer what they understood, not only whether they liked it. Refine your guide as your business learns.
