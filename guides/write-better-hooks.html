CONTENT CREATION

Write a hook they can’t scroll past.

By Pocket AI · Creator’s Notebook

Start with a specific promise

A good hook helps the right person recognize that a piece of content is for them. Instead of “Here are some content tips,” try “Three ways to plan next week’s posts in twenty minutes.” Make the benefit concrete, realistic, and connected to the actual content.

Create curiosity, not confusion

Give your audience enough context to care. A useful pattern is problem, surprise, payoff: “Your posts might not need better design. They might need a clearer first sentence. Here’s how to fix it.” Avoid manufactured urgency and promises you cannot keep.

Write five versions before choosing one

Try a question, a direct benefit, a personal observation, a common mistake, and a practical checklist. Read them aloud. Pick the one that sounds natural and gets to the point fastest. The Hook Generator can help you explore alternatives, but your judgment should make the final call.

Test what happens after the click

A high click rate is not enough if people immediately leave. Compare watch time, meaningful comments, and saves. Keep the promise made in your opening, and change one element at a time so you can learn what actually helped.
