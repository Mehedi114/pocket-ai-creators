WORK SMARTER

Turn one good idea into a week of content.

By Pocket AI · Creator’s Notebook

Choose an idea with real substance

Start with a question your customers genuinely ask. Write a useful answer with an example, a mistake to avoid, and an action someone can take today. That original answer becomes the source for the week, not a set of seven disconnected posts.

Adapt the format, not just the length

On Monday, publish the full answer as a short article. On Tuesday, turn the main lesson into a carousel. On Wednesday, film a quick demonstration. On Thursday, share the mistake and its fix. On Friday, invite your audience to ask follow-up questions.

Keep a simple content map

For each post, note the audience, takeaway, format, and call to action. A video needs a spoken opening; a carousel needs a visual sequence; an email needs a clear subject line. Repurposing should make the idea native to each platform, not copy and paste it everywhere.

Leave room for your audience

Batch drafts, but do not automate away the conversation. Review comments and adjust the next post to address the questions people actually ask. Use the Content Calendar tool as a planning assistant and leave flexible slots for timely ideas.

Keep creating

  • Write a hook they can’t scroll past.
  • Find a brand voice that sounds like you.
