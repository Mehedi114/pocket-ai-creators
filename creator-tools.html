Do the AI tools require an API key?
AI mode needs a working server-side provider. A configured provider can still fail due to network access, quota, authentication or model restrictions. Local prompt and tag modes use templates and rules, not an AI model. Never enter API keys into these forms.
Are financial results professional advice?
No. They are planning estimates under the assumptions displayed. Tax rules differ by country and personal situation. Check with a qualified adviser before making financial decisions.
Will every PDF get smaller?
No. Structural optimization cannot guarantee savings, particularly for scanned documents. Password-protected files are not supported. Always keep your original and review signatures, forms and formatting after any PDF operation.
