How to Remove a Plain Photo Background — Colour & PNG Guide

Prepare a photo taken against a simple, single-colour backdrop without sending it to an image service. Pocket Helper samples a corner colour and removes similar pixels connected to the image boundary. You can correct the colour selection and adjust tolerance before exporting.

Open Remove Background ↗

Read the limits below, then open the tool to get started.

How to use Remove Background

  1. 01

    Upload or drop a JPG, PNG or WebP photo with a plain backdrop.

  2. 02

    Check the preview, pick the original background colour if needed, and adjust tolerance. Choose transparent, a preset colour or a custom colour.

  3. 03

    Compare Original and Edited, then download PNG for transparency or JPG with a solid background.

A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A computer shop can prepare a product photo taken against a white wall, remove that plain backdrop and export a transparent PNG for a flyer. Inspect pale edges and fine details before using it.

Important limits

This is colour-based, edge-connected removal, not AI subject segmentation. Hair or clothing similar to the backdrop can be removed. Complex scenery is not supported. Input is limited to 15 MB and 24 megapixels; editing is reduced to at most 2000 pixels on the longest side.

Common questions

Is this an AI background remover like remove.bg?

No. This tool is designed for plain, single-colour backdrops. It does not identify a person or object with an AI segmentation model. For complex backgrounds, use an appropriate segmentation editor.

What should I check before using the result?

This is colour-based, edge-connected removal, not AI subject segmentation. Hair or clothing similar to the backdrop can be removed. Complex scenery is not supported. Input is limited to 15 MB and 24 megapixels; editing is reduced to at most 2000 pixels on the longest side.
