Important limits

Maximum input is 15 MB and 16 megapixels; output dimensions are capped at 2048 pixels per side. Only a still image is exported; animation and most metadata are not preserved. Smaller files are not guaranteed.

Common questions

No. It adds no Pocket AI watermark, but does not remove marks already present in the pixels. Respect image ownership and compare the converted image before publishing.
