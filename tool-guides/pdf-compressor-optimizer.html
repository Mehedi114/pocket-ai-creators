A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A PDF written with uncompressed structural objects may shrink after optimization. An already-optimized scan may not, because most of its size can be embedded images.

Important limits

There is no guaranteed compression ratio. The tool does not downsample images. Rewriting can affect signatures, forms or other document structure; keep your original and inspect the output.

Common questions

Why did my PDF not get smaller?

It may already be optimized, or its size may mostly come from image data this workflow does not recompress. The tool then offers the original unchanged rather than claiming fake savings.

What should I check before using the result?

There is no guaranteed compression ratio. The tool does not downsample images. Rewriting can affect signatures, forms or other document structure; keep your original and inspect the output.
