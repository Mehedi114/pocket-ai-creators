Put it to work

For revenue of 10,000 and expenses of 2,000, profit is 8,000. At a 25% reserve rate, the tool sets aside 2,000 and shows 6,000 remaining.

Important limits

This is not a US, UK or other country's tax return calculator. It omits brackets, credits, social contributions and VAT. Loss scenarios are not modeled. Seek qualified advice when choosing the rate.

Common questions

Does this calculate my exact tax bill?

No. It applies the flat rate you enter to estimated profit. Actual liability depends on jurisdiction and personal circumstances.

What should I check before using the result?
