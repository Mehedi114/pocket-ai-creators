A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

With 60,000 take-home income, 10,000 expenses, a 25% reserve and 1,500 annual billable hours, the estimated rate is 60 in your selected currency.

Important limits

The reserve is modeled as a flat percentage of profit. This does not calculate national taxes, VAT, benefits or market demand. All entered amounts must use the same currency.

Common questions

Should I enter every hour I work?

Use billable hours, not total time at your desk. Sales, bookkeeping, training and unpaid administration reduce the hours available to invoice.

What should I check before using the result?
