How to Make a Passport Photo Print Sheet — Sizes & PDF Guide

Prepare a photo for a printing workflow with explicit dimensions rather than guessing its size on a page. Choose a common preset or custom pixels, position the crop and generate a PDF sheet with copies arranged at their intended physical size. Always follow the receiving institution's instructions.

How to use Passport Photo & Print

  1. 01

    Upload a photo, choose a size preset and adjust zoom, position or rotation to frame the subject.

  2. 02

    Check the background and photo requirements. For an official application, avoid retouching or background edits unless expressly allowed.

  3. 03

    Download a single JPG or PNG, or choose a sheet size and copy count for a PDF. Print the PDF at Actual size / 100%.

A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

Arrange six 35×45 mm photos on a 4×6 inch sheet. The layout reserves margins and gaps; if the requested number does not fit, the tool asks you to reduce the count instead of silently shrinking the photos.

Important limits

Passport and visa requirements vary by country and application. This tool does not verify head size, expression or official acceptance. Input limit: 15 MB and 24 megapixels; editing source is capped at 2000 pixels. Custom output is 50–2400 pixels per side and print dimensions use 300 DPI. A target JPG file size may require smaller dimensions; it is not guaranteed at every resolution.

Common questions

Does a 35×45 mm preset guarantee passport acceptance?

No. A dimension preset only sets the output shape and print size. Check the official rules for background, head position, recency, expression and permitted editing before submitting.
