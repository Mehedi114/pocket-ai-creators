A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A larger down payment reduces the financed principal. Shortening the term generally increases the monthly principal-and-interest payment while reducing total interest, assuming the same fixed rate.

Important limits

This is an amortization estimate, not a lender quote. It does not independently calculate closing costs, mortgage insurance, property tax or HOA fees. Extras entered are excluded from total loan repayments.

Common questions

Is the interest rate the same as lender APR?

Not necessarily. This model divides a nominal annual interest rate by 12. Lender APR may include fees and use rules that the calculator does not model.

What should I check before using the result?

This is an amortization estimate, not a lender quote. It does not independently calculate closing costs, mortgage insurance, property tax or HOA fees. Extras entered are excluded from total loan repayments.
