A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A query value such as “summer sale & gifts” needs encoded spaces and an encoded ampersand when inserted as one parameter. This prevents the ampersand from being treated as a separator.

Important limits

This tool encodes a component, not an entire navigable website URL. It does not shorten links. Literal plus signs remain plus signs during decoding; this is not form-urlencoded decoding.

Common questions

Why does my complete URL get encoded slashes?

Component encoding treats slashes and punctuation as data. Use the result as a component value, not as a replacement for an entire URL.
