A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A restaurant can encode its own menu URL and place the downloaded SVG on a printed table card. The QR destination remains the original menu address, not a Pocket AI tracking link.

Important limits

These are static QR codes: you cannot change the encoded destination after printing. The code has no Pocket AI expiration, but the linked page must remain available. It does not shorten URLs or provide scan analytics.
