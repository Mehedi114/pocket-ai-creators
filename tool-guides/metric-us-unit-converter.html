Put it to work

One mile equals 1.609344 kilometers, while 32 degrees Fahrenheit equals 0 degrees Celsius. These are different formulas: temperature conversion includes an offset rather than a simple ratio.

Important limits

Volume uses US customary gallons, fluid ounces and cups, not Imperial measures. Displayed results are rounded. Temperatures below absolute zero are rejected.
