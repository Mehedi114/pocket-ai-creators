Free Profit Margin Calculator — SaaS & E-commerce

Keep margin and markup distinct when reviewing a product or subscription business. Margin measures profit relative to revenue; markup measures profit relative to costs. Enter all costs relevant to the measure you want.

A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

Revenue of 100 with total entered costs of 60 gives profit of 40, a 40% margin and approximately 66.67% markup. These percentages are not interchangeable.

Important limits

The tool cannot know costs you omit. It does not independently calculate tax, churn, customer acquisition cost or refunds. Zero cost makes markup undefined; negative profit indicates a loss.

Common questions

Does the result show gross or net margin?

That depends on which costs you include. Enter direct costs for a gross-style measure or all relevant allocated costs for a more comprehensive estimate, and label your result accordingly.

What should I check before using the result?
