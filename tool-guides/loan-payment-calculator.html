A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A 1,200 loan with zero interest over one year has a monthly payment of 100. At positive interest rates, part of each payment covers interest on the outstanding balance.

Important limits

Fees, irregular payment dates, early repayments and changing rates are not modeled. The rate is divided into monthly periods. Your lender's exact schedule may differ.
