How to Make an Assignment Cover — A4 Layout & Export Guide

Create a clean cover page for a university or college assignment without laying out every line manually. Pocket Helper offers three white-paper designs, restrained accent colours and separate submitted-by and submitted-to sections. Type Bengali or English details and review the live preview before downloading.

How to use Assignment Cover Maker

  1. 01

    Choose Classic, Modern or Editorial, pick an accent colour and select Bengali or English cover labels. Optionally upload your institution logo.

  2. 02

    Enter the institution, assignment topic, course and code, student name and roll, teacher details and submission date. Leave optional fields blank when unnecessary.

  3. 03

    Review spelling and layout, then download A4 PDF or PNG. Use Actual size / 100% when printing and follow your institution's required format.

A PRACTICAL EXAMPLE

Put it to work

A Management student can add their name, roll, session, course title, assignment topic and lecturer details, then export a restrained navy cover on white A4 paper. Load sample demonstrates the layout; replace all sample details before submission.

Important limits

This creates a cover, not the assignment content. PDF output contains a high-resolution image and is not editable text. Name, institution and topic are required for export. Long text must fit the layout; shorten it if warned. Optional logo input is limited to 3 MB and 12 megapixels. Details are not saved automatically, so reloading clears the form.

Common questions

Can I use Bengali text on the cover?

Yes. The cover uses locally hosted Bengali fonts and supports Bengali or English labels. Wait for fonts to load before exporting. The design is not an official university template; use the prescribed format if your department requires one.
