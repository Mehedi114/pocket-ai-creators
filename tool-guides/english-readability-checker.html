Important limits

Syllables and sentence boundaries are heuristic. Names, abbreviations, very short texts and non-English writing can produce misleading scores. Grade level refers to an approximate US school-grade formula.

Common questions

Does a high reading-ease score mean the article is good?

No. It suggests simpler surface language under the formula. It does not measure factual accuracy, structure, empathy, relevance or whether readers understand the material.
