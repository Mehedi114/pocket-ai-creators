How to use Face Retouch

Make small portrait adjustments while keeping control of the result. Pocket Helper provides local face detection to position a softening area, brightness and shadow controls, and a manual brush that blends a small blemish toward the surrounding colour. Original view makes comparisons easy.

  1. Upload a portrait. Wait for local face detection, or select the face centre yourself if no face is found.
  2. Adjust light and softening gradually. Enable the spot brush and click a small blemish; Undo removes the most recent spot correction.
  3. Compare Original and Edited, inspect skin and eye detail, and download a PNG or JPG.

Before you download

This is not Photoshop, skin reconstruction or an identity-photo validator. The brush blends neighbouring colour rather than restoring missing detail. Detection may miss faces; with multiple faces, the largest is selected. Input limit: 15 MB and 24 megapixels. Editing is capped at 2000 pixels; the smaller interactive preview does not set export dimensions. Do not use retouching where passport or ID rules prohibit it.
