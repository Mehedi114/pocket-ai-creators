How to use Assignment Cover Maker

Create a clean cover page for a university or college assignment without laying out every line manually. Pocket Helper offers six white-paper designs, restrained accent colours and separate submitted-by and submitted-to sections. Type Bengali or English details and review the live preview before downloading.

  1. Choose Classic, Modern, Editorial, Minimal, Arch or Scholar, pick an accent colour and select Bengali or English cover labels. Optionally upload your institution logo.
  2. Enter the institution, assignment topic, course and code, student name and roll, teacher details and submission date. Leave optional fields blank when unnecessary.
  3. Review spelling and layout, then download A4 PDF or PNG. Use Actual size / 100% when printing and follow your institution's required format.

Before you download

This creates a cover, not the assignment content. PDF output contains a high-resolution image and is not editable text. Name, institution and topic are required for export. Long text must fit the layout; shorten it if warned. Optional logo input is limited to 3 MB and 12 megapixels. Details are not saved automatically, so reloading clears the form.
